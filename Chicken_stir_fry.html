Chicken Stir Fry

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 25 mins

Total Time: 40 mins

Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 500 g chicken breast, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 cup broccoli
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger

Steps:

  • 1. Slice the chicken breast into thin pieces and mix with soy sauce and sesame oil.
  • 2.Slice the onion, capsicum, and carrot, and cut the broccoli into small pieces.
  • 3. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden and cooked through.
  • 4. Add the onion, capsicum, carrot, broccoli, and garlic. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until the vegetables are slightly tender.
  • 5. Mix the soy sauce, honey, and ginger in a small bowl.
  • 6. Pour the sauce over the chicken and vegetables. Stir well and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  • 7. Serve the chicken stir-fry hot with rice or noodles.
