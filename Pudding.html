Pudding

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 30 mins

Total Time: 40 mins

Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 2 cups milk
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps:

  • 1. Make the caramel: Add the sugar and water to a small saucepan over medium heat. Cook until the sugar melts and turns golden brown.
  • 2. Prepare the moulds: Carefully pour the caramel into four small heatproof pudding moulds and let it cool slightly.
  • 3. Make the custard: Warm the milk and sugar in a saucepan over low heat until the sugar dissolves. Do not let the milk boil.
  • 4. In a bowl, gently whisk the eggs and egg yolks. Slowly pour in the warm milk mixture while stirring.
  • 5. Pour the custard through a fine sieve to make it smooth.
  • 6. Divide the custard between the moulds.
  • 7. Place the moulds in a deep baking dish and add hot water around them until it reaches about halfway up the sides. Bake at 160°C for 25–30 minutes
  • 8. Remove the moulds from the water and allow the puddings to cool.
  • 9. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving. Run a knife around the edge and turn the pudding onto a plate.
