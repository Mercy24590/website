Mac and Cheese

Prep Time: 10 mins

Cook Time: 20 mins

Total Time: 30 mins

Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 250 g macaroni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 cups grated cheddar cheese
  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Steps:

  • 1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions until tender. Drain and set aside.
  • 2.Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the flour and stir for about 1 minute.
  • 3. Slowly pour in the milk while stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
  • 4. Continue cooking until the sauce thickens, then add the grated cheese. Stir until the cheese has completely melted.
  • 5. Add the salt and black pepper and mix well.
  • 6. Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and stir until all the pasta is coated.
  • 7. Serve the mac and cheese while hot.
