Chocolate Chips Muffins

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 30 mins

Total Time: 45 mins

Servings: 12 muffins

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps:

  • 1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line a 12-cup muffin tray with paper liners.
  • 2. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  • 3. In another bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  • 4. Slowly add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and gently mix until just combined. Do not overmix.
  • 5. Fold the chocolate chips into the mixture.
  • 6. Divide the mixture evenly between the 12 muffin cups.
  • 7. Bake for 18–20 minutes, or until the muffins are golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  • 8. Remove the muffins from the oven and let them cool in the tray for a few minutes.
  • 9. Transfer the muffins to a cooling rack and allow them to cool before serving.
